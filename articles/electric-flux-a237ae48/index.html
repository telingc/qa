Electric flux & Gauss' Law

Electric Flux

Electric flux measures the electric influence to an area of the field.

Φ=∮Eda=qϵ0\Phi=\oint\textbf{E}\text{d}\bf{a}=\frac{q}{\epsilon_0}Φ=∮Eda=ϵ0​q​
With the superposition principle, we have:
E=∑iEi\textbf{E} = \sum_i\textbf{E}_iE=i∑​Ei​
thus we have the Gauss' law:
∮Eda=Qencϵ0\oint\textbf{E}\text{d}\bf{a}=\frac{Q_{enc}}{\epsilon_0}∮Eda=ϵ0​Qenc​​
Notice, Gauss' law is insanely elegant under symmetric situations when E\text{E}E is constant. For less symmetric situations, the surface integration is non-trivial.
