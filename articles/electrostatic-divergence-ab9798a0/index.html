Divergence of Electrostatic Fields

The Differential Form of Gauss' Law

Throughout this note, we assume that E\mathbf EE is continuously differentiable and ρ\rhoρ is continuous in the region under consideration. The differential form follows pointwise because the integral identity holds for every sufficiently small volume VVV with a piecewise smooth boundary in this region.
