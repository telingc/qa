## Capacitance
Capacitance is a purely geometrical quantity, determined by the sizes, shapes, and separation of the two conductors. The definition of capacitance:$$C\equiv\frac{Q}{V}.$$And as you should notice that even this is the definition, the capacitance isn't determined by the total charge $Q$ and the difference of the potential $V$, or the potential of a single conductor itself.

For cases of fixed conductors, the capacitance remains unchanged, only determined by the sizes, shapes, and separation of the two conductors.
## Energy
Since we've discussed the potential difference and its relation with work, we have $\displaystyle V(\mathbf a)-V(\mathbf b)=\frac{W}{Q}$, for the continuous situation, we need the differential element of $W$: $$dW=q\,dV=q\,d\frac{q}{C}=\frac{q}{C}dq.$$Then the integral: $$W=\int_0^Q\frac{q}{C}dq=\frac{1}{2}CV^2$$
