## Electric Flux
Electric flux measures the electric influence to an area of the field.$$\Phi=\oint\textbf{E}\text{d}\bf{a}=\frac{q}{\epsilon_0}$$With the superposition principle, we have:$$\textbf{E} = \sum_i\textbf{E}_i$$thus we have the Gauss' law:$$\oint\textbf{E}\text{d}\bf{a}=\frac{Q_{enc}}{\epsilon_0}$$Notice, Gauss' law is insanely elegant under symmetric situations when $\text{E}$ is constant. For less symmetric situations, the surface integration is non-trivial.
