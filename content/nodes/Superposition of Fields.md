# Fields and Forces
We know the superposition of forces, the sum of the forces proportional to the sum of fields:$$\textbf{F}=\sum_i\textbf{F}_i=\sum_i\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{\text{q}_i\text{Q}}{\textbf{r}^2}\hat{\textbf{r}}=Q\sum_i\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{\text{q}_i}{\textbf{r}_i^2}\hat{\textbf{r}_i}=Q\textbf{E}$$It's trivial to predict the continuous situation:$$\textbf{E(r)}=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\int\frac{\rho(\tau')}{r^2}\hat{\textbf{r}}\text{d}\tau'$$
