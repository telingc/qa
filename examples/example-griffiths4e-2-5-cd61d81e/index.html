Charged Ring & Charged Disk

  1. Find the electric field a distance zzz above the center of a circular loop of radius RRR that carries a uniform line charge λ\lambdaλ.
  2. Find the electric field a distance zzz above the center of a flat circular disk of radius RRR that carries a uniform surface charge σ\sigmaσ. What does your formula give in the limit R→∞R\to\inftyR→∞? Also check the case z≫Rz\gg Rz≫R.

1. Circular Loop

Intuitively, we can say that there's no horizontal field on the point, for this conclusion we have an complete explain of its symmetry. For now we take this as known, thus we only take vertical field into consideration. By drawing the graph, we can infer that:

d E⊥=14πϵ0λRz⋅dθ(R2+z2)3/2⇒E=14πϵ0∫02πλRz⋅dθ(R2+z2)3/2=λRz2ϵ0(R2+z2)3/2d\,E_\perp=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{\lambda Rz\cdot d\theta}{(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}}\Rightarrow E=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{\lambda Rz\cdot d\theta}{(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}}=\frac{\lambda Rz}{2\epsilon_0(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}}dE⊥​=4πϵ0​1​(R2+z2)3/2λRz⋅dθ​⇒E=4πϵ0​1​∫02π​(R2+z2)3/2λRz⋅dθ​=2ϵ0​(R2+z2)3/2λRz​

2. Circular Disk

To calculate circular disk which is an area version of the circular loop, we're using the surface charge σ\sigmaσ.

d E⊥=14πϵ0dqs2⋅cos⁡θ n^, cos⁡θ=zs, dq=σr⋅dθdr, s=r2+z2d\,\mathbf{E}_{\perp}=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{dq}{s^2}\cdot\cos\theta\,\hat{\mathbf{n}},\,\cos\theta=\frac{z}{s},\,dq=\sigma r\cdot d\theta dr,\, s=\sqrt{r^2+z^2}dE⊥​=4πϵ0​1​s2dq​⋅cosθn^,cosθ=sz​,dq=σr⋅dθdr,s=r2+z2​
