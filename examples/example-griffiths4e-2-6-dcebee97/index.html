Spherical Surface

  1. z<Rz<Rz<R, inside the spherical surface.
  2. z>Rz>Rz>R, outside the spherical surface. Use direct source integration and express both results in terms of the total charge q=4πR2σq=4\pi R^2\sigmaq=4πR2σ.
